## Extraction and review

Two PDF reading orders were merged by source page, column and question number. Option labels with missing spaces and clearly separated bare labels were recognized. Headings were removed before extraction. Instructions introducing the next shared scenario were separated from the preceding option. No corrections to business facts or answer inference are implied.

All IDs and normalized question-plus-option fingerprints are unique. Option order is ignored; case, numbers and punctuation are retained. 0 exact duplicate occurrences were removed after merging extraction passes. 2 similar-stem pairs are listed for review. Differently worded duplicates may remain.

245 records are flagged for shared-context review, 10 for visual review, 24 for calculations or notation, and 14 for historical context. These groups overlap. Short stems are conservatively flagged because names and phrases such as “the document” can refer to an earlier scenario. Flags are heuristic and do not replace checking every record.

Each record links to its source PDF page and raw column text. Check adjacent columns and pages for continued questions, tables, balance sheets, documents and shared business scenarios.
