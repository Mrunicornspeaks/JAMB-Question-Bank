# 1,000 English draft records

500 existing records preserved verbatim; 500 additional records: 245 stand-alone lexical/sentence-interpretation questions and 255 comprehension questions linked to 55 passages.

english_1000.csv and english_1000.json contain all 1,000 questions. english_passages.json stores shared passages. english_print_part_01.csv through english_print_part_10.csv provide 100-row print sections; comprehension questions in these print sections require their passage from english_passages.json. The main CSV/JSON include passage text directly.

Validation: 1,000 unique IDs and fingerprints, nonempty four/five-option sets, CSV/JSON record counts agree, all original 500 preserved, comprehension passage references resolve. Automated checks do not establish semantic uniqueness, source authenticity or answer accuracy. PDF transcription errors may remain. All answer fields are blank and require review.
